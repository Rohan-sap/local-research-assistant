Local AI models run on hardware controlled by the user or organization.

One advantage of local AI is that prompts and documents can remain within the local environment instead of being sent to an external AI service.

Local AI can require significant computing resources depending on the size of the model.

Cloud AI services can provide access to powerful models without requiring users to own the underlying AI hardware.

Cloud services may introduce ongoing usage costs and require data to be transmitted to an external provider.

The appropriate choice between local and cloud AI depends on requirements such as privacy, model capability, hardware availability, cost, and operational complexity.
